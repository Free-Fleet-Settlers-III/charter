# Charter of the Free Fleet

## I. Prologue & Core Tenets:

1. Vow of Sovereignty:

    All signatories hereby vow to protect and respect the sovereignty of all other signatories involved, be it persons or guilds. The signatories also recognize the base values of freedom, charity, goodwill, and mercy to be of high importance.

1. Membership:

    The following clauses apply to all Free Fleet members (i.e. signatories) equally. Failure by member guilds and persons to ratify this Charter, and maintain committment to this document will result in a vote of non-confidence and potential removal from the ranks of Free Fleet. 

1. Free Trade and Freedoms:

    Member guilds cannot be restricted by the Fleet Assembly in maintaining free intrafleet (domestic) trade. Fleet member guilds or persons may not be vassals, and may not be subjects in any manner or form that may impact their independence and participation in Freefleet alongside it's obligations. Fleet members may only hold vassals at the agreement of the Fleet Assembly.

1. Foreign relations:

    Signatories and members agree to cooperate in their foreign relations and policy activities and only perform it in accordance to common sense, goodwill, and this charter. Signatories also agree to an obligation of reporting towards Fleet Assembly and the Chief Diplomat, so as to avoid conflicting information and tangled relations.

1. Resources, common good, mutual aid:

    Signatories agree to strive for the common good of the Fleet through sharing a reasonable portion of resources and labor amongst themselves, so as to further the collective benefit and advancement of the Fleet as a whole.

1. Relational levels, status:

    Freefleet is structured in a five tiered system of status. Signatories to this document are at it's highest level, that of member guild, able to delegate Aldermen, etc. The second level is associate guild, which are guilds that are in some kind of relation with another guild or faction that is preventing them from becoming full members. Third level is that of allied guilds, who are in an official alliance with Free Fleet, but not signatories. Fourth level is Amicable or Unofficially Allied guilds, which are friendly, but do not have an officiated alliance with Free Fleet, perhaps only a handshake agreement. Fifth level is that of Observant Parties, these are neutral parties which may be traders, reporters, diplomats, but have no formal or informal relation with Free Fleet.

## II. The Systems of Governance

Introitus:  
Free-fleet from the day of ratifying this document shall operate as a federation comprised of sovereign parties sworn to each other's aid and side. By the nature of this formation, the members of the Free-fleet Federation are equals no matter their size or political weight, thus provided by this charter is an equal right for representation, and equal votes.

Interpretation guidance for the following clausures:  
All votes detailed within this clausure are counted by number of attended voters, i.e. non-attendees are not counted as nay votes, and a vote may pass even if attendance is subpar. In order to still provide legitimacy, non-wartime, or non-urgent votes have 24 hours to be attended to.

### 1. Fleet Assembly, Aldermen

1. The [fleet assembly] is made up of one representative from each signatory, member guild, who bear the title of [aldermen]. 
1. The [alderman] title is only to be naturally granted to the guildmaster of the member guild. 
1. If the guildmaster loses their guildmaster title, the [alderman] position can be still retained, so long as their represented guild holds a vote to retain and agrees with a supermajority (2/3).
1. [Aldermen] may propose a vote, or vote on any decision without restriction of legislative topic. (Anything standard other than war is a 1/2 vote) The [magistrate] may veto and send back for reconsideration, this applies to all votes.
1. [Aldermen] may vote regarding the granting of additional titles,  such as [minister], [marshall], [justiciar], and [emissary].
1. [Aldermen] may be subjected to a vote of non-confidence in justified and fair cases.
1. The [Fleet Assembly] may be convened at any location and time in accodance to the [aldermen]'s preference.

### 3. Marshall

[marshall]: #3-marshall

1. The [marshall] is a title reserved only for wartime organization.
1. The [marshall] title is granted by the [fleet assembly] through a vote in complete agreement. (i.e. full veto)
1. The [marshall] may cast emergency decrees aimed to produce quick action results in wartime.
1. The [marshall] may be called back any time through a supermajority vote. (2/3)

### 4. Chief Diplomat & Magistrate

[magistrate]: #4-chief-diplomat-magistrate

1. This is an unelected position, which at the time of signing is held by Arkes (Avensur). This title Will be referred to as [Magistrate] in the rest of the document.
1. This title may be inherited at the discretion of the previous title holder by anyone suggested through supermajority vote for appointment by the [aldermen].
1. The [magistrate] may proposition, in the [fleet assembly], a vote of non-confidence aimed at any official of governance. This vote requires a higher than supermajority vote, but is not a full veto vote. (4/5 of votes)
1. The [magistrate] may suggest to the assembly [Justiciars] and [Emissaries] for appointment under him as aides. (1/2 vote)
1. The [magistrate] may call the [Justiciars] and [Emissaries] back any time at discretion and based on fair cause.
1. The [magistrate] acts as a highest court of appeal, and as mediator in cases of internal conflict and cases to be judged.
1. The [magistrate] may delegate specific cases to his subordinates.
1. The [magistrate] may enact emergency measures in fair and reasonable cases.
1. The [magistrate] may veto the [Fleet Assembly] votes in fair and reasonable cases.
1. The [magistrate] is the sole holder of the right to ammend this charter, others may suggest ammendments.

## III. War

Freefleet's Independent and leage-like structure comes with difficulties regarding the waging of wars. The following clausure's aim to alleviate such issues, but preserve the sovereignty of all signatories.

### 1. Defensive war

[defensive war]: #1-defensive-war
[defensive wars]: #1-defensive-war

1. Any invasion, attack, act of terrorism, declaration of war, and blockade against any of the signatory guilds by a foreign party is considered to be an act of war against the whole of Free Fleet.
1. In accordance to this, Free Fleet members have an obligation to protect, and provide for the aggrieved party, and participate as if they themselves have been dealt the blow. 
1. All signatories agree to a mutual defense pact by signing this document, and are obliged follow this clausure.

### 2. Marines, Mariners, Stockpiles

[marines]: #2-marines-mariners-stockpiles
[mariner]: #2-marines-mariners-stockpiles
[mariners]: #2-marines-mariners-stockpiles

1. Signatories agree to facilitate the founding of a [Mariner] Militia or Corps within Free Fleet, which will act as a standing defensive army and navy for the whole of the fleet. It's rules are to be established in another document.
1. Signatories also agree to facilitate training of these [Mariners] through joint combat excercises and practice aimed at achieving combat proficiency.
1. Signatories agree to [stockpile] weapons and armor to provide at the least 2 sets of armor and weaponry for each member (type and quality can be agreed upon in Assembly), and a further 1 set for half of their populace, to provide for the alleviating defender guilds' reinforcements. (Common sense applies)
1. Every signatory guild shall keep atleast one ship that is armed with cannons and ready to be used in case of war.

### 4. Offensive war

[offensive war]: #1-offensive-war
[offensive wars]: #1-offensive-war

1. [Offensive wars] may only be waged upon a complete agreement, i.e. a full veto vote by the [Fleet Assembly].
1. [Offensive wars] mandatorily require the assigning of a [Marshall], as detailed above.

## IV. Joining and Leaving

### 1. Joining

1. The joining of guilds may be overseen by the [Fleet Assembly] and [Magistrate], and decided upon outside of a vote.
1. In cases of faliure of discussions or controversy, a vote may be held within the [Fleet Assembly].
1. The former two points apply to the joining of individuals, however, discussion may not necessarily be held.

### 2. Leaving

1. Signatory guilds may leave Free Fleet of their own accord, however, their populations must hold a vote, where an 4/5 majority must be reached for leaving.
1. Signatory individuals may leave at their own discretion, but are asked to report reason for leaving upon departure.
